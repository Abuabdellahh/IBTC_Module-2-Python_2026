    🌿 Our Story

    From a family kitchen in Bole to Addis Ababa's favourite delivery.

    How It Started

    Addis Eats was born in 2019 when Tigist Haile started cooking her mother's recipes for neighbours in Bole. Word spread fast. Within six months, what began as a weekend side project became a full kitchen serving hundreds of orders a week.

    Today we're a team of 12, still cooking every dish from scratch with the same recipes — no shortcuts, no compromises.

    Our Values

    • 🌿 Authenticity — every recipe is traditional, every ingredient is sourced locally.
    • 🤝 Community — we source from local farmers in Oromia and Amhara regions.
    • ♻️ Sustainability — biodegradable packaging, zero food waste policy.
    • ❤️ Inclusion — full fasting menu available every day of the year.
